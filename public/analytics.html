Where archived thermal candidates recur

Original 2023–2025 FIRMS pixel centers are grouped offline into 0.01° WGS84 research bins. This grid is neither a sensor footprint nor an event boundary. A bin seen on multiple dates is a repeated candidate; the potential-static label is an unvalidated review cue based on long recurrence and FIRMS's own source type. It does not confirm an industrial site, non-vegetation source or wildfire. Empty bins have unknown satellite opportunity.
